## How the pixel art was made

Since v0.5.0 the heroes, backgrounds and animations were generated with [PixelLab](https://www.pixellab.ai), an AI
pixel-art tool, from our own written descriptions and references, under the [PixelLab terms of
service](https://pixellab.ai/termsofservice). Every sprite was then selected, edited and assembled
by hand: faces redrawn, heads pasted across animation frames, artefacts cleaned, colours of Mykyta's fur recoloured for
each world. The license above applies to these images to the extent the authors hold rights in them. The first version
of the art, drawn entirely in code, is covered by the Apache License 2.0 together with the code.

## Public-domain sources

Characters and settings are based on works in the public domain: Ivan Kotliarevsky, Ivan Franko, Lesya Ukrainka,
Mykhailo Kotsiubynsky, Hryhorii Kvitka-Osnovianenko and Ukrainian folk tales. The public-domain texts themselves are not
covered by this license. Visual designs are original and are not derived from film or cartoon adaptations.
